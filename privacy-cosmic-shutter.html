Privacy Policy

Effective: September 3, 2026 Applies to: the Cosmic Shutter Android app Contact: cosmicshutter@santoryulabs.com
In short
  • Cosmic Shutter collects no personal data, of any kind, ever.
  • The app works fully offline — it never connects to the internet.
  • There's no account, no login, no analytics, and no ads.
  • Nothing you enter ever leaves your phone.

Cosmic Shutter is a scorekeeping companion for the Cosmolancer tabletop game, built by a fan for other players at the table. This page explains, plainly, what the app does and doesn't do with your information — because "we don't collect anything" still deserves to be written down properly.

1.Data we collect

None. Cosmic Shutter does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on any server, because it has no server to send anything to. There is no sign-up, no email address requested, no device identifier read, and no third-party SDK embedded in the app that could do any of this on our behalf.

2.What happens to the scores you enter

Player names, tile placements, and round scores you enter during a game are held only in the app's memory while you're playing, and optionally saved to your device's local storage so you can resume a game later. That save lives on your phone alone — Cosmic Shutter has no way to read it back, copy it, or send it anywhere. Uninstalling the app removes it, same as with any other local file.

3.Permissions

The app requests no runtime permissions — no camera, no contacts, no location, no storage access beyond its own local save file. If a future update ever needed a new permission, that request would explain, at the moment you grant it, exactly what it's for.

4.Analytics, ads, and tracking

There are none. No analytics library, no ad network, no crash-reporting service phoning home, no cookies (the app isn't a website), and nothing resembling a tracker of any kind.

5.Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data from anyone, this holds equally for players of any age, including children. There's simply nothing being gathered for the policy to restrict.

6.Changes to this policy

If Cosmic Shutter ever changes in a way that touches this policy — say, an online feature is added down the line — this page will be updated first, with a new effective date above, before that change ships.

This is an independent, non-commercial fan project for the Cosmolancer board game. It isn't affiliated with, endorsed by, or produced by the game's publisher.

7.Questions

Write to cosmicshutter@santoryulabs.com with anything this page didn't answer.
